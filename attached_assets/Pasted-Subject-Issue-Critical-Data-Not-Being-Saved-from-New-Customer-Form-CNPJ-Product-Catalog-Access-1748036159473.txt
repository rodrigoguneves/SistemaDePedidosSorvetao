Subject: Issue: Critical Data Not Being Saved from New Customer Form - CNPJ & Product Catalog Access Settings

Description:
We've identified an issue where essential data points are not being correctly registered or saved to the database when a new customer is created via the 'New Customer' form in the administrator panel. This prevents proper setup of customer accounts and their specific purchasing abilities.

Specific Data Failing to Register/Save Correctly:

CNPJ (Tax ID): The CNPJ entered into the form for the new customer is not being saved with the customer's record.
Assigned Product Categories: The product categories selected by the administrator to be accessible by the new customer are not being correctly associated with the customer's account (e.g., no records created in the CustomerAllowedCategories table or equivalent).
Allowed Sale Units per Assigned Category: For each product category assigned to the customer, the specific sale units (e.g., 'Unit', 'Half Box 12pcs') that the administrator selects as permissible for that customer within that category are not being saved (e.g., no records created in the CustomerCategoryAllowedSaleUnits table or equivalent).
Expected Behavior:
When an administrator creates a new customer:

The CNPJ provided in the form must be saved to the Customers table (or equivalent tax_id field).
All selected product categories that the customer should have access to must be correctly linked to the customer's account.
For each of those assigned product categories, all selected allowed sale units must also be correctly linked to the customer for that specific category.
This data should then be accurately reflected when viewing/editing the customer and when the customer logs in to view their product catalog.
Area of Impact:

Administrator Panel: New Customer Creation (Página Novo Cliente / Create New Customer Form).
Data Integrity: Affects Customers, CustomerAllowedCategories, and CustomerCategoryAllowedSaleUnits tables.
Customer Portal: Customers will not see their correct product catalog or available purchasing options.
To Be Verified:

Ensure the backend API endpoint for creating a new customer correctly receives and processes these fields (CNPJ, selected categories, and selected sale units per category).
Ensure the database schema correctly supports these relationships and that data is being inserted into the respective tables (Customers, CustomerAllowedCategories, CustomerCategoryAllowedSaleUnits).
Ensure the frontend form correctly submits all selected data for these fields.
Priority: High, as this directly impacts core B2B functionality and customer setup.
